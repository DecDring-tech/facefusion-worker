| Part | Length | What |
|---|---|---|

| TJR | 7.0–7.5 s | Regera / Bugatti shots, "Wrong tax bracket lil bro" etc. |

## What changed from round 5 (why it was off beat)

- **Round 5 cut on detected transients, not the beat.** Hi-hats and vocal syllables count as transients, so cuts were
  landing on random hits at uneven spacing and it read as off-beat. Round 6 only cuts on strong onsets, with a cost per
  cut and a minimum shot length of one full beat of that track, so cuts sit on the kicks and snares with 0.5–1.3 s shots.
- **Best drop in each sound**, scanned across the whole track. In every sound except Bannu the first drop was already the
  biggest. For Bannu the biggest is at 6.12 s (your own post switched on the third-biggest at 8.98 s), so that one moved.
- **Sound start trimmed so the bait is 3.0–3.7 s** (Bannu starts 2.82 s in, 404oskar 3.0 s, USE HEADSET 2.80 s,
  North Pole 1.8 s, luxuryxsh 1.5 s, GS Group 0.6 s).
- **Better B-roll.** Added the Car Week spotter clip of the Regera in traffic (banner and watermark cropped off) and the
  Bugatti clip; removed every shot that was near-black, that carried a sign or caption, or that ended on a head close-up.
  Long shots are split into separate windows so nothing is slowed or frozen.

## Rebuilding

```
python3 pipeline/ttdl.py <post id> out.mp4     # download a public TikTok post, no watermark
python3 pipeline/grid.py                       # tempo + drop per sound -> grid.json
python3 pipeline/build6.py out_dir             # renders every entry in specs_round6.json using plan6.json
python3 pipeline/verify6.py                    # offset of every placed cut to the nearest onset
```
`build6.py` expects the clips in `r6clean/` and the sounds in `sounds_dl/` next to the scripts.
