# How to make a viral GTA 6 streamer clip (the formula I use before every cut)

## 1. Find the moment (what to scan the VOD for)
Scan chat spikes, clip counts and the streamer's own volume. Keep a moment only if it is one of these, in priority order:
1. **Drama / stakes** between the streamer and friends (calls, arguments, "he's not coming", bets).
2. **First time / forbidden**: first strip club, first cop chase, first break-in, first death, first gun fight, first boat/plane.
3. **Humbled by the game**: NPC fights back, cops instantly, car explodes, falls off something.
4. **Hot take about GTA 6**: map size, graphics, Lucia, "better than GTA 5?", price, loading.
5. **Pure fail**: plane crash, drowning, wrong button, wasted cutscene.
6. **Wholesome / soft**: puppy, dad joke, "he's having fun again", gift to chat.
7. **Fanbase in-joke**: Fortnite language for Clix, Kai/AMP references for YourRAGE, sister banter for Lauren.
Rule: the moment must have a visible PAYOFF inside 20 seconds (a crash, a scream, a face, a line). No payoff, no clip.

## 2. Cut
- Start **1 second before the setup line**, end **1 to 2 seconds after the payoff**. Target 14 to 25 seconds. If the payoff is at 8 seconds, end at 12 and ship a 12 to 15 second clip. Shorter beats longer.
- Never open on silence or a loading screen. The first frame must show the streamer's face already reacting or the game already moving.
- If the moment needs context, write the context into the headline, do not include it in the footage.

## 3. Frame (1080x1920)
- **Top ~40%**: face cam, cropped tight on the head and shoulders, scaled to full width.
- **Bottom ~60%**: gameplay, cropped from the centre of the 16:9 stream so the action is in frame.
- **Headline across the seam**: 2 to 3 lines, Inter ExtraBold or Impact style, white with black outline (or white box with black text). Streamer's name is the first word. One word in CAPS or yellow for the emotion.
- **Word captions** under the headline on the gameplay side when the streamer talks: 1 to 3 words at a time, bold, white with outline, colour accent on the punch word.
- No logos, no watermark, no borders.

## 4. Headline template (pick one)
- `[NAME] [EMOTION-CAPS] [what happened] in GTA 6 😭`  →  "Clix DISAPPOINTED with the SIZE of the GTA 6 map 😳"
- `[NAME]'s [thing] goes wrong`  →  "Lacy's puppy encounter goes wrong"
- `[NAME] [does forbidden thing] for the FIRST time`  →  "Clix enters the GTA 6 strip club for the first time 😭😳"
- `[NAME] tries to [crime] and gets [humbled]`  →  "clix tries to break into someones house and gets owned by 2 ladies"
- `[NAME] [called/told] [friend] after [stakes]`  →  "Clix called Ron after finding out he's no longer going to Miami"

## 5. Caption + hashtags template
`[2 to 6 word joke or question] [1 to 3 emoji] #[Name] #gta6 #[name]clips #fyp #streamer`
- Lacy: must include **#LACY** and his name. Example: `Lacy did NOT mean to do that 😭 #LACY #gta6 #lacyclips #fyp`
- Clix: must include **#Clix** and his name. Example: `Clix vs the whole neighbourhood 😭 #Clix #gta6 #clixclips #fyp`
- YourRAGE: name + **#YourRAGE**. Example: `YourRAGE found the one NPC with hands 💀 #YourRAGE #gta6 #fyp`
- Lauren Hamden: **#LaurenHamden** + tag **@LaurenHamden**, posted from the dedicated account with the bio link. Example: `Lauren's first 5 minutes of GTA 6 😭 @LaurenHamden #LaurenHamden #gta6`
- Ask a question when there is a debate: "Is the map too small?" "Should Ron go?"

## 6. Pre-post checklist (30 seconds)
1. Name in headline AND caption. 2. Campaign hashtag present. 3. 14 to 25 s. 4. First frame = face or action. 5. No logo. 6. English. 7. Post, copy link, submit on Content Rewards within 30 minutes.

## 7. Per-streamer moment guide
- **Lacy**: he crashes out and over-reacts (he literally won "biggest crash out award"). Look for screams, slamming the desk, arguing with Marlon/Jason/Clix in the mansion, IRL Miami missions between game sessions, puppy/animal moments.
- **Clix**: Fortnite-brain reactions (calls everything "endgame", "200"), rage at NPCs, disappointment takes (map size, graphics), strip club/forbidden curiosity, phone calls with Ron/Lacy.
- **YourRAGE**: dry one-liners and dead-pan disbelief, long pauses then an explosion, talking to chat like they're in the room, refuses to pause, references to Kai Cenat / AMP / his dog Creed.
- **Lauren Hamden**: new to the game, so first-time confusion is the content (controls, driving, dying), sister Katie in the background or on call, mods/chat telling her what to do ("LET HER PLAY"), soft wholesome reactions. Keep it flattering: her campaign rejects anything negative.
