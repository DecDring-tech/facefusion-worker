## D. Patterns that fell out of the data

1. **Face cam top half, gameplay bottom half, headline across the seam** is the format on 13 of 15 TikTok winners. Nobody is winning with gameplay-only.
2. **Headline = full sentence with the streamer's name first** ("Clix DISAPPOINTED with…", "Lacy's puppy encounter goes wrong"). Name first satisfies the campaign rule and tells the fan whose clip it is before they read anything else.
3. **One CAPS word or emoji carries the emotion**: DISAPPOINTED, FIRST, STR!P, FATAL, 😭😳🥹.
4. **Length**: the best view-per-second clips are 7 to 20 seconds. 14 to 25 is the safe window. Over 40 seconds only works for a drama story (clip #1).
5. **Topics ranked by what travelled this week**: (1) drama between the CORE boys about the marathon, (2) first-time/"forbidden" locations (strip club, breaking into houses), (3) NPC humbles the streamer, (4) disappointment/opinion about the game (map size, graphics), (5) fails and deaths (plane, car, drowning), (6) wholesome (puppy, having fun again), (7) in-jokes for the streamer's existing fanbase (Fortnite "200", "endgame").
6. **Speed**: the same moment posted hours later gets 10x fewer views (#3 vs #12). Post the moment the stream produces it.
7. **Captions**: 2 to 6 words, lowercase or a question, 2 to 4 emoji max, hashtags: #streamername #gta6 #fyp + the campaign tag. Questions ("Should Ron go?", "Who's in Miami?") drive comments.
8. **No logos / clean frame**: winners have zero watermarks, which is also the campaign rule.
